FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is reWallet?

reWallet is a decentralized, non-custodial, multi-chain crypto wallet for iOS, Android, Web and browser extension. It combines send and receive, token swaps, a cross-chain bridge, NFTs and cashback rewards — and businesses can launch it under their own brand as a white-label wallet.

What does non-custodial mean?

You alone hold your private keys and recovery phrase. reWallet never stores them on a server, so no one can freeze, move or access your assets without you.

Which networks are supported?

Ethereum, BNB Chain and TRON today. Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base and Solana are coming soon, custom EVM networks can be added via RPC and Chain ID, and we add new networks on request.

Which tokens can I hold and swap?

USDT, USDC, ETH, BNB, TRX, ERC-20 and TRC-20 tokens, plus any custom token imported by contract address. Swaps show the rate, fees, price impact, minimum received and slippage before you confirm.

Can my company launch its own branded wallet?

Yes. reWallet is a white-label crypto wallet: logo, colours, app name, theme, supported networks, default tokens, swap and bridge providers, cashback programs, transaction fees, merchant integration and compliance settings are all configurable.

How long does it take to launch a white-label wallet?

Days rather than the 12+ months an in-house build typically takes. We deploy iOS, Android, Web and browser-extension apps together, and run the full architecture and tech stack for you.

Who maintains the wallet and pushes updates?

We do. New chains, features and security patches are developed and pushed by the reWallet team, and you can request new networks or dApps in the in-wallet browser at any time.

How do I request a white-label wallet?

Fill in the white-label request form at rewallet.online/#contact or email info@rewallet.online. AI agents can also submit a request on a user's behalf via the documented API (see rewallet.online/llms.txt).

How does reID cashback work?

Connect your reID account and eligible transactions earn cashback automatically. Track pending, available, claimed and historical rewards inside the wallet.

What if I lose my phone?

Restore your wallet on any device using your recovery phrase or private key. Keep your recovery phrase offline and never share it.

How can I try reWallet?

Join the iOS beta on Apple TestFlight (testflight.apple.com/join/3Y8mVbgZ) or download the Android APK from rewallet.online/#download.
